What makes a neighborhood walkable?

Ran several modeling approaches — clustering, association rule mining, Naïve Bayes, SVM, logistic regression, and a 200-tree Random Forest — across 220,000+ US census block groups to find what actually separates walkable neighborhoods from car-dependent ones. Every method agreed: transit access is the single biggest predictor.
