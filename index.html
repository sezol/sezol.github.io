Walkability across America — EPA National Walkability Index

Ran five different modeling approaches — clustering, association rule mining, Naïve Bayes, SVM, logistic regression, and a 200-tree Random Forest — across 220,000+ US census block groups to find what actually separates walkable neighborhoods from car-dependent ones.
